# WE GOT AN UPDATE!

Hey everybody! Just wanted to update you guys on the whole Mii modeling journey.

I know my original approach to the situation was to make a specific program or workflow with Arian to make the possibilities of exporting Miis to all kinds of model formats.

The model formats being: 
- DAE 
- FBX
- MDL

and much more! 
___
# Curiosity kills the cat... or something. 
___
Well I got curious and logged into Tower Unite recently with my friends, and started looking for examples of Mii models. 

![Kat21's Mii Model](/images/kat21miimodel.png)
I came across this model from none other than the creator of Mii Creator [kat21](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3475581212)!

I then thought of the bright idea of asking him how I could export the Miis and make them work with Tower Unite. 

He gave me some guidance of where to get started, and I was mostly on the right track, except for a few mistakes, but hey I'm new to this. 
___
# Issues...
___
One issue I was running into was the mii model looking weird when I exported to DAE and imported into Tower: 
![DAE messup!](/images/DAE.png)

The textures weren't importing, due to the files for each texture not being named properly. 

Once I got that all situated, I went ahead and imported it into Tower Unite, where, I ran into the issue of the face mask...

This was the preview in Blender. 
![Blender preview](/images/blender-preview.png)

I honestly thought I was cooked, but thankfully Tower Unite has options for modifying the metadata and look of textures. I was able to use transparency for the textures and all was good! 

I went ahead and rendered a test to see how it would look for the DAE model. I used the material preview mode, not solid/workbench mode, thanks to Kat for letting me know, lol. 
![Mii Render with material](/images/mii-render-with-material.png)
___
# And after all that... 
___
And then it was time to import it into Tower Unite, I went ahead and opened the game once again and nagivated over to the Workshop model editor, and sure enough I imported it, and got the masking problem resolved, and was able to upload it! 
![Imported to Tower Unite](/images/imported-to-tower-unite.png)
The hoodie looks like a void in the lighting of Tower Unite, I'm not sure whether or not there could be a fix for that or not. I'll just leave it alone for now.
