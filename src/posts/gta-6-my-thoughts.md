# The hype is real!
___
So quite literally everybody and their grandma is talking about the new release coming out in a couple of hours as I'm typing this: Grand Theft Auto VI! 

While it is supposed to be out for everybody all across the world at midnight on November 19th, many streamers have done the "New Zealand" method.

They essentially switch their location to New Zealand to allow themselves to start playing as early as 5:00 AM CST today! 
___
# My Thoughts So Far
---
I am curious to see how the common people will look at the newest addition to GTA and what they have to say about it, whether or not it met their expectations.
___
# Retrospective of GTA V
___
I can say for myself I've enjoyed the story mode for the previous Grand Theft Auto game, which was GTA V.

I'm also looking forward to how they're going to do the online side of things for GTA 6. I definitely had a lot of fun playing GTA V Online with my friends, whether it was just driving around Los Santos or doing heists, or just messing around with mods... it was lots of fun, and definitely made some core memories with my old friend group through that game that I still think back to this day.

###### Miss goofing off with this dude lol
___

I remember back a few years ago, me and my old friend would just hop on GTA V and just talk about what's going on in our lives while we would drive aroudn Los Santos.

But now that's in the past, and I'm gonna start focusing on the present which brings us to GTA VI. 

I personally think it's a bit overhyped. They didn't start development 13 years ago. They started development in late 2022. There's a lot of bugs that are being found in the first few hours of gameplay from the "New Zealand" streamers.
___
# My Verdict
___

I don't think I'm really gonna pay much attention to GTA VI until the PC release is out.

I ditched my XBOX One back in 2024 and went fully on the PC train since I know I can do just about anything with a computer lol. 

But for now, I'm going to be going through the story mode of GTA IV and Liberty City Stories on PC while I wait.
